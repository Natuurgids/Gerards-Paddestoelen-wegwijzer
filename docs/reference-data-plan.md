# Reference data expansion

Taxonomy is versioned against the Catalogue of Life Base Release. Biological character descriptions and determination mappings are only added from cited mycological sources; unknown characters remain unmapped. Localized content may fall back to the scientific name or an available source language rather than inventing vernacular names or translations.

Images require per-file provenance, creator and licence metadata before they are bundled. Generated/schematic determination aids are explicitly educational and are not species-identification evidence on their own.
